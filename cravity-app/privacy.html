Location Services
We collect your Real-time Geo-data only when the app is active to find nearby restaurants and enable the "Eat With Me" beacon feature. This data is ephemeral and not stored permanently.

Payment Security
All transactions are encrypted via AES-256 bit SSL. Cravity does not store your raw credit card numbers; they are tokenized by our payment processor (Stripe/Razorpay).

Social Sharing
Your "Beacon" status is only visible to Accepted Friends. You can toggle "Stealth Mode" in the Social Radar settings to become invisible instantly.

Cookies & Cache
We use local storage to remember your "Dark Mode" preference and "Cart Items". No third-party tracking cookies are used for advertising.
